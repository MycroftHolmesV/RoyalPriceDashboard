# Artwork provenance

The Home Assistant App assets were generated specifically for Royal Price
Dashboard with OpenAI ImageGen on 2026-08-27, then mechanically reduced with
Lanczos resampling to the Home Assistant presentation sizes:

- `icon.png`: 128 x 128, transparent PNG
- `logo.png`: 250 x 100, transparent PNG

No cruise-line, Home Assistant, or upstream-project artwork was supplied as a
reference. The prompts explicitly excluded those trademarks.

## Icon prompt

> Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: Home Assistant App icon. Create one
> original, vector-friendly flat icon for an open-source app named Royal Price
> Dashboard. Primary visual: a simplified generic cruise ship bow rising above
> two clean ocean wave curves, integrated subtly with a small downward
> price-trend line ending in a dot. Composition: centered square mark, bold
> readable silhouette, generous safe padding, legible at 48px and 128px.
> Palette: deep midnight navy and bright aqua/teal with a small cool-blue
> accent. Background: transparent. Mood: trustworthy, calm, modern, useful.
> Constraints: no text, no letters, no crown, no anchor-and-crown combination,
> no Royal Caribbean or Celebrity logos, no recognizable cruise-line funnels
> or trademarks, no Home Assistant logo, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no
> photorealism, no watermark. Deliver a polished single icon, not a sheet or
> mockup.

## Logo prompt

> Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: horizontal Home Assistant App logo. Create
> one original, polished horizontal logo for an open-source app. On the left,
> use a compact simplified generic cruise-ship bow over two ocean wave curves
> with a small downward price-trend line; match a deep midnight navy, bright
> aqua/teal, and small cool-blue accent palette. On the right, render the exact
> words "Royal Price Dashboard" in a clean, bold geometric sans-serif, on two
> balanced lines if needed, with excellent readability around 250 by 100
> pixels. Composition: wide 5:2 transparent canvas, generous outer padding,
> symbol about one third of width, text visually centered beside it. Style:
> vector-friendly flat branding, crisp shapes, restrained detail. Constraints:
> exact spelling only; no extra text; no crown; no anchor-and-crown combination;
> no Royal Caribbean or Celebrity logos; no recognizable cruise-line funnels
> or trademarks; no Home Assistant logo; no mockup; no photograph; no
> watermark. Deliver a single logo on transparency.
